Privacy Policy

Malayalam English Bible (Sathyavedapusthakam 1910 & KJV) · Android app com.sathyavedapusthakam.bible
Effective 7 October 2026

This app does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not use the internet at all.

What stays on your phone

Your bookmarks, highlights, notes, reading-plan progress and settings (language, theme, font) are saved only on your device. The developer never receives them.

Backups

  • Backup files you make. When you choose Saved → Back up, the app writes a file to a location you pick. The app does not send it anywhere; what happens to it after that is up to you.
  • Android's own backup. If Android backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include this app's data in your personal Google account backup, as it does for most apps. That is handled by Android and Google under your Google account, not by this app, and the developer cannot see it.

Links to websites

Word Study has buttons that open a word's page on Bible Hub or Blue Letter Bible. They open in your own web browser, not in this app; those websites have their own privacy policies. The app sends nothing to them.

Sharing verses

When you share a verse as text or as a picture, the app hands it to the app you choose (for example, a messaging app) using Android's share menu. Nothing is sent unless you choose to share.

Crash reports

If the app crashes, Google Play may give the developer anonymous technical crash reports (Android vitals), which Android collects for all apps. They do not identify you. The app itself contains no crash-reporting or analytics code.

Permissions

The app asks for no special permissions: no internet, location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage access.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
